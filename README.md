<h1>Before You Build: Is Your Organization Actually AI-Ready?</h1>

<div class="pull">"Most AI product failures don't happen at the idea stage — they happen because the organization wasn't set up to support what it was building."</div>

<p>This course covers your role, your team, and how to communicate AI's value. But there's a foundational layer beneath all of that: <strong>operational readiness</strong> — whether your organization is genuinely prepared to integrate and sustain an AI-powered product.</p>

<h2>Four Pillars of Readiness</h2>
<div class="grid">
  <div class="box"><div class="num">01</div><div class="btitle">People & Skills</div><p class="bdesc">Do the right roles exist? Any gaps needing hiring or upskilling?</p></div>
  <div class="box"><div class="num">02</div><div class="btitle">Process Clarity</div><p class="bdesc">Who reviews AI outputs? What's the plan when the model is wrong?</p></div>
  <div class="box"><div class="num">03</div><div class="btitle">Technology Fit</div><p class="bdesc">Does existing infrastructure support the AI component?</p></div>
  <div class="box"><div class="num">04</div><div class="btitle">Stakeholder Buy-In</div><p class="bdesc">Who still needs convincing? Skepticism at senior levels can derail initiatives.</p></div>
</div>

<p>Three of the four pillars are non-technical. Operational readiness is largely a people and process challenge — which puts it squarely in the AI PM's court.</p>

<div class="callout">
  <p>📊 <strong>Data Point:</strong> McKinsey's State of AI research finds that top-performing AI organizations are differentiated not by the technology they use, but by formal governance, cross-functional teams, and documented deployment processes.</p>
</div>

<h2>A Quick Self-Check for Your Team</h2>
<ul class="checks">
  <li>If the model makes a wrong prediction, what's our process to catch and correct it?</li>
  <li>Which stakeholders are still unconvinced — and what would change that?</li>
  <li>What does success look like at 6 months, and who owns measuring it?</li>
</ul>
